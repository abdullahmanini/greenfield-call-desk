        How this was built
        
    
        Method and limits
        
    
        I built this to practise the work of a cloud sales development rep: research an account, find a reason to call, open in thirty seconds, ask good questions, hand clean notes to a seller, and keep calling until the meeting is booked.
        
    
        Where the accounts come from
        
    
        
    
          
      
        • Each account has one public event (an expansion, acquisition, funding round, new digital service, leadership hire, grant or contract) dated between  and . The event links to the press release or news story it came from.
          • 
      
        • Companies were left out if their own public material says they are customers or partners of a major cloud provider, if they sell IT services, or if they have more than about 5,000 employees.
          • 
      
        • Employee counts appear only where a source gives one. Otherwise the page says "unknown".
          • 
      
        • The page names job titles to call, never individual people. The only names shown are executives named in the event itself.
          • 
    
        
    
        How the call sheets are written
        
    
        The opener, discovery questions, objection, voicemail and email are built from the event type and the account's own event. They are starting points to rehearse and rewrite, not finished scripts. The "hypothesis to test" is a guess about the business problem, written to be confirmed or dropped in the first conversation.
        
    
        What this page does not know
        
    
          
      
        • Which cloud provider, if any, each company uses today, and whether it already has a sales relationship with one.
          • 
      
        • Any internal CRM data, territory assignment or qualification method used by a real sales team.
          • 
      
        • Real call results. The dial log holds only what you enter, in this browser. Until you log a block it shows a made-up sample week, labelled as such.
          • 
    
        
    
        Tools
        
    
        Built with Claude Code. Research used public web sources, each checked against its page. The page is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no libraries.
